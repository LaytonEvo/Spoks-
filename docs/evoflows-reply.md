## Answers to your three questions

### 1. Can the app export a pack as your flow JSON? **Yes: we'll compile it on our side.**
Packs are already structured data, not prose. Each pack (`dashboard/app/drafts/<id>.json`) holds a step graph in Klaviyo's
flow-API shape: `actions[]` with `temporary_id`, `links.next` / `next_if_true` / `next_if_false`, `time-delay`
(`value`, `unit`, `delay_until_time`, `timezone`), `conditional-split` with a `profile_filter`, `send-email` with subject,
preview, sender and a template key, `send-sms`, `update-profile`, `back-in-stock-delay`. The outline text is only the human summary.

We'll add an `evoflows` target to `klaviyo/build_packs.py` that emits your schema directly: graph → nested
`split.branches[].steps[]` (our branches never re-join; Klaviyo forced us to duplicate tails, which maps cleanly to your tree),
delays → `wait`, splits → `when` conditions, emails → `email` steps with template keys. Then `POST /flows/{key}/validate`
in CI before anything is pushed. You don't need to accept our outline format.

What we need from you for the compiler: the condition field list confirmed (see "points to settle" 2, 3 and 6).

### 2. Can we pull current template HTML out of Klaviyo? **Not needed: the source of truth is in this repo.**
Every email is generated by Python builders (`klaviyo/design/build_f1.py`, `build_f2.py`, `build_f3.py`, `build_f6.py`,
`build_f9.py`, `build_f12.py`, `build_f13.py`), output to `dashboard/app/drafts/html/<pack>/<key>.html`. The hand edits
Layton made in Klaviyo after QA (F1 badge, F3 copy fixes) are already mirrored in the builders. (Reading flow templates
back out of Klaviyo also works; editing them doesn't.)

Rather than regex-translating Klaviyo tags, the builders already render two flavours from one source (`ctx.live` =
Klaviyo tags, preview = sample data). We'll add a third flavour that writes **Go-template tokens** natively. That's cleaner
and safer than translating `{% if %}` blocks after the fact. Please start from our HTML, not the `emails/` sources in your repo,
so the copy Layton has approved is what ships.

### 3. Which custom events exist today? **None.**
Klaviyo's metric list (checked today) has only Shopify events (Placed/Fulfilled/Cancelled/Refunded Order, Checkout Started,
Added to Cart, Viewed Product/Collection, shipment events), Klaviyo internal events (email/SMS/form events,
Subscribed to Back in Stock) and API on-site events. **No membership event from the portal or the membership app.**

Membership today is only state, not events:
- Klaviyo profile property `MemberTier` (values seen: `AnnualMember` 642, `Club`, `Evolution Pro`; **no `Free`**), source
  unconfirmed.
- Shopify customer tags: `AnnualMember` 743, `EvoMember` 613, `ClubhouseMember` 358, `clubhouse-member` 57, `Club`, `Evolution Pro`.
- The theme runs the **Appstle Memberships** app embed, and the portal is `members.evolutiongolf.co.uk` (Stripe). One of those
  is the real source and should push `membership_changed` to `POST /events`. Luke to confirm which.

These two disagree (~210 profiles with the annual tag but no annual `MemberTier`, ~75 the reverse; the Free tier isn't
tagged anywhere we can see). **This has to be settled before F2, and before any flow that branches on member status**,
on either platform. It's on Layton's checklist with Luke, section 1–4.

## Points to settle before you build

1. **Image hosts.** Your push rule allows images only on `cdn.shopify.com` / `evolutiongolf.co.uk`. Our templates use
   Klaviyo's CDN (`cdn.klaviyomail.com`, `d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net`) for the logo, photos, member card and icons
   (~30 files). They'll stop being ours when Klaviyo is switched off anyway. **We'll re-host them on Shopify Files** and
   switch the builders. Fine with your rule; just flagging that every current template would fail validation today.

2. **Conditionals inside templates.** We use template-level conditions heavily, not just flow splits: member tier
   (annual / Free / non-member wording), what's in the order (`"Motocaddy Electric Trolleys" in event.Collections`),
   brand, and first-name fallbacks. In Go templates that needs a list-contains helper. **Does Listmonk's transactional
   renderer expose Sprig (`has`, `default`)?** If not, please add `contains`/`default` funcs, or we have to move every
   conditional block into flow-level `else_template` variants (many more templates).

3. **Collections: titles vs handles.** Our conditions use collection **titles** as Shopify sends them to Klaviyo (e.g.
   "Motocaddy Electric Trolleys", 38 names in `build_f3.py` / `build_f9.py`). Your `order.collections` is **handles**, and
   your F9 example uses `golf-trolleys`, which isn't a collection on the store. We'll map titles → handles from Shopify
   when compiling. Please expose the same lists to templates (`.Tx.Data.order.collections`) so template and flow agree.

4. **Your F9 example isn't the approved F9.** The approved one is v3 (`f9-after-delivery-v3.json`, live as a Klaviyo draft):
   - credit offer only if the order is ≤10 days old at day 3;
   - the reminder at day 10 only if the order is ≤12 days old **and they still aren't a member** (new re-check);
   - reviews at ~2 weeks after dispatch for trolleys and ~4 weeks for clubs;
   - till (POS) orders excluded;
   - Motocaddy content decided by a Motocaddy trolley collection anywhere in the order;
   - PowaKaddy switched off.

   Our compiler will emit the exact version. Treat yours as illustrative only.

5. **Opens and clicks are not in v1. That blocks F12 and F13 on Evoflows.** F12 Winback's segment needs "opened or clicked in
   the last 180 days" (to avoid mailing the unengaged). F13 Sunset is entirely about "no opens/clicks in 150 days" and
   "exit on click". Until your pixel + redirect ship (phase 2), **F12 and F13 should stay on Klaviyo or wait**. Also needed then:
   `contact.last_open_at`, `contact.last_click_at`, `contact.emails_received`.

6. **Fields we use that aren't in your list:**
   - `customer.member_tier` with values agreed (annual / free / none), once the source is fixed (above);
   - `order.vendors[]` is listed, good (brand content);
   - `contact.sunset_status` or a generic `attributes.*` (F13 tags non-responders; `set` covers writing it);
   - "ordered in the last N days" as a condition at a later step (F9 credit offer / reminder): `customer.days_since_last_order`
     covers it if evaluated at step time, not trigger time. **Please confirm conditions are evaluated when the step runs.**

7. **Smart sending / frequency cap.** Klaviyo's Smart Sending (skip if they had an email in the last 16h) is on for most of
   our emails and deliberately off for F2 Annual's welcome, F6's back-in-stock alert and the F3 member path. Your engine
   guarantees don't mention a cross-flow cap. **Do you have one?** If not, we'd like `"skip_if_emailed_within_hours": 16`
   per email step, with `0` to bypass.

8. **Unsubscribe and preferences.** Fine that the engine injects unsubscribe. Our footers also have **manage preferences**
   (`{% manage_preferences %}`). Is there an Evoflows/Listmonk equivalent (`.Tx.Data.preferences_url`)? If not, we'll drop
   that link.

9. **SMS out of scope:** noted. Affected steps: F1 (welcome text), F3 (day-2 text on all three paths), F6 (4-hour text).
   They'll be dropped when compiled for Evoflows; the emails stand alone.

10. **Back in stock (F6)** has no Evoflows trigger. The Klaviyo button and "Subscribed to Back in Stock" go when Klaviyo goes.
    Options: keep F6 on Klaviyo, or a `custom` event from whatever replaces the button (the Notify Me app is at its plan
    limit). Layton's call.

11. **Re-entry values to carry over:** F3 7 days, F9 30 days (set by hand in Klaviyo today), F12/F13 once per person.
    Your F9 example says 60 days.

12. **Send windows.** Our waits use exact send times (09:30, 17:30, 18:00). Your `until_time` covers it; check that
    `quiet_hours` 21:00–08:00 doesn't shift any of them (it shouldn't).
