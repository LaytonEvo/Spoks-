Evolution Golf flow map

Every automated flow we're building, in build order: who gets in, who's kept out, when they leave, what they receive and when, and what each one replaces. Nothing here is switched on; every new flow is a draft until you turn it on in Klaviyo.

Rules that apply to every flow

One conversation at a time

Priority runs checkout → cart → browse → welcome. Starting a checkout takes people out of the cart, browse and welcome flows so they only hear about their basket.

Buying ends the selling

An order takes people out of every "come back" flow (welcome, checkout, cart, browse, winback) and starts the after-purchase flows.

No bounced addresses

Anyone whose email bounced in the last 30 days is kept out of everything, to protect your sender reputation.

Spacing

Smart sending is on (Klaviyo won't send two marketing emails within 16 hours). Texts respect quiet hours and only go to people who agreed to texts.

Members are members

Members never get public codes. Where a flow speaks to everyone, members see a version that points to their own 10%.

Two groups

Flows that split by product use two paths: Hardware (trolleys, clubs, used clubs) and Everything else.
