Email platform decision:
Klaviyo, Spoks or an alternative

For discussion between Layton and his business partner · 28 September 2026 · prepared with Claude from hands-on tests of Klaviyo and Spoks, plus web research on alternatives

1. The decision

We have a complete build pack for a new email and SMS flow programme — 14 flows covering welcome, membership, abandonment, post-purchase, winback and sunset. It was written for Klaviyo. We are testing whether to build it somewhere cheaper, using Claude connected to each platform to do most of the work.

The question: stay on Klaviyo, move to Spoks, or trial another platform first?

3. Klaviyo

Pros

  • Best-looking emails for the least effort: Claude writes the designs and reusable blocks.
  • Everything in the build pack works as written: splits, smart sending, bounce filters, A/B subject tests, emails sent “from Alex”.
  • No migration risk: list, consent, SMS and history already in place.
  • The existing flows can be edited in place.

Cons

  • Flow wiring is manual: ~14 flows in the Klaviyo UI.
  • Cost: ~$720/month, around 20× Spoks. Suppressing unengaged profiles lowers it.
  • Klaviyo’s raw API can now create flows (beta) but not edit them or add A/B tests; the connector Claude uses can’t, so flows stay manual in practice.

4. Spoks

Pros

  • Much cheaper: $35/month, no send cap.
  • Claude builds all the flows: triggers, delays, “next day at 09:30” timing, audience filters.
  • Membership tier tags already sync from Shopify, so membership flows can trigger directly.
  • Abandonment emails show the customer’s real basket with a link back.
  • Brand colours and fonts are already applied.

Cons

  • Limited design: no HTML/code and no design import.
  • Reusable blocks built and placed by hand.
  • Gaps: no per-email “from Alex” sender; no bounce filter; no A/B subject tests via Claude; tag-triggered flows enrol a customer only once; category paths become separate flows.
  • Go-live work: paid plan, sender and domain, SMS set-up, explain the 6k vs 46k contact gap.

5. Tests we ran that didn’t work

  • Spoks components via Claude: not possible — Spoks components can only be built by hand.
  • Designing visuals in Canva for Spoks: quality wasn’t good enough; dropped.

What this means

  • No platform we could confirm lets Claude build both the flows and the designs. Everyone else splits the work like Klaviyo or Spoks.
  • Omnisend is the one to check. If its connector really builds automations, it could automate both halves with good Shopify coverage at a mid price. Needs a free-trial test first.
  • Brevo is a cheaper Klaviyo. Claude builds the designs; we wire the flows. Thinner Shopify link, and its discount codes share one fixed expiry date rather than “7 days from sending”, which clashes with our coupon rules.
  • MailerLite is cheap and AI-buildable, but too thin on Shopify for our membership tiers and category routing.
  • Everything else costs more than Klaviyo for less, or can’t be built by an AI at all.
