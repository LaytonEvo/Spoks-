Choosing an electric trolley

Four questions get you most of the way. Range: 18-hole lithium suits most golfers; go 36 if you play twice on a Saturday. Boot: will it fold in next to your bag? Hills: downhill control matters more than any gadget. GPS: nice to have, but a phone app does most of it.
