Before this goes into Klaviyo

  1. Welcome email 1: your current 5% email makes most of the welcome money. The draft uses the new membership email; a copy of your current one (with the "expires" wording taken out) sits next to it as a template so you can swap it in.
  2. Choosing E2: people who looked at trolleys or clubs in their first day get Hardware; everyone else gets Everything else. Klaviyo can't tag interest from link clicks here, so it uses what they browsed.
  3. Send times: the flow tool can't set "send at 09:30". Set the time of day on each wait in the Klaviyo editor.
  4. Starting a checkout: anyone who starts a checkout leaves this flow, so the basket emails take over. (The plan said "pause for 2 days"; Klaviyo can't join paths back together, so pausing would mean 16 copies of the same emails.)
  5. Timing: E1 straight away, SMS 1 hour later, E2 the next day, E3 two days after that, E4 three days after that.
  6. Still to confirm: the phone number in Alex's email, and the wording for how used clubs are checked.
  7. Trustpilot figure: 4.8 from 611 reviews is written into E1 and E4, so it needs re-checking every quarter.
