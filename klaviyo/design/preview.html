      Four things the designs surfaced

      1. Gold, not yellow. Your logo's accent is a warm gold (#B2893F). The Build Pack's bright yellow (#F1DA01) isn't in any of your live assets, so all three options use the logo gold for small accents. Say if you want the yellow kept.
      2. "FREE GIFT" badge in product photos. The basket block shows each product's main Shopify photo. For the M1 DHC that photo carries a "FREE GIFT" roundel, which would keep showing after the offer ends. The mock-ups use the clean second photo; in the live email, the product's main photo needs to be badge-free.
      3. Lifestyle photos need to be bigger. The photo in option B is from your Klaviyo library and is only 490px wide, so it will look soft on phones and retina screens. For option B we'd want originals at least 1200px wide. Your own shots of the shop, fitting bay or team would beat manufacturer imagery.
      4. Web fonts only show in Apple Mail. Fraunces and Inter load on iPhone and Mac Mail. Gmail and Outlook fall back to Georgia and Arial. Use the Fonts switch to see both.
